# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with strict TypeScript, as specified in `task.md`.

## Users

The primary user is the administrator of a barber shop, using a mobile-first web application to manage the business.

## Product Purpose

Webbys is a frontend-only administrative PWA for managing a barber shop. The first delivery establishes the login simulation, mobile navigation, and business dashboard; later stages add operational modules. Success means the administrator can navigate a coherent demonstration without real credentials or backend services.

## Positioning

The product brings the administrator's barber shop operations into one interface, with a shared schedule intended to respect each barber's working hours, breaks, days off, and existing appointments.

## Operating Context

The interface is designed primarily for phone use, with layouts adapting to larger screens. All first-stage names and metrics are demonstration content, not claims about a real business.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend only; do not create a backend, real authentication, database, or real notification delivery.
- The initial delivery covers simulated login, responsive navigation, and dashboard.
- All visible interactions in the delivered scope must behave coherently with demonstration data.
- The broader product brief and later modules are specified in `task.md`.
- Barber-shop display name: Webbys.

## Evidence on Hand

No real business data, logo, or brand assets have been provided. The current W monogram and app icons are authored demo assets, not official supplied branding. Dashboard data must be labeled as demonstration data; do not invent business performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Keep the administrator's common work easy to reach on a phone.
- Make simulated behavior explicit and never imply backend operations occurred.
- Keep demonstration data coherent across the interface.
- Keep schedule rules centralized when the agenda module is implemented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide readable contrast, accessible form controls, and comfortable touch targets as required by `task.md`.
